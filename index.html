FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Modozo, how it works, and how it helps fashion teams streamline product development and production.

What is Modozo?
Modozo is a fashion product lifecycle management platform. It brings tech packs, BOMs, samples, approvals, vendor communication and production tracking into one workspace, so brands and manufacturers work from the same current information.
How does Modozo reduce manual work?
Structured tech packs replace scattered files, and updates, approvals and comments are recorded against the style. Tools powered by AI, such as Techpack PDF to Excel, remove retyping, so teams spend less time copying data between documents and chasing status.
Can I collaborate with vendors?
Yes. Invite vendors into a shared workspace where they can access the tech packs, samples and orders you choose, comment in real time and confirm costs, without exchanging files over email or chat.
Does Modozo support sample and production tracking?
Yes. Every sample round is tracked against its specification, and production orders are followed through fabric, cutting, stitching, QA check and dispatch, with measurement, workmanship, colour and packaging checks recorded.
How does Modozo keep everyone aligned during product development?
Every team works from a single versioned record per style. Changes, comments and approvals update for all roles, including brand, design, fit, sourcing, vendor and QA, so nobody builds from an outdated file.
Is my product data secure?
Product data lives in a central workspace with access set by role, so each team member and vendor sees only the styles and information shared with them. For details on hosting and compliance, contact the Modozo team.
Who is Modozo built for?
Apparel brands, manufacturers and sourcing teams that manage products from concept through production and delivery, together with the design, fit, sourcing, vendor and QA teams who work on them.
